First steps

Install Dewline, write a note, keep or revert a change, then open your own folder.

Install Dewline

Download the disk image and drag Dewline into Applications, or use Homebrew:

$ brew install --cask ark-232/tap/dewline

Dewline needs macOS 26 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel. It is free and has no account. The cask also puts the dewline command on your PATH.

Write your first note

  1. Open Dewline. It makes a Dewline folder in Documents and opens Welcome.md there. The caret waits at the end of the note.
  2. Type anything. Dewline saves as you go. There is no Save prompt.
  3. Press ⌘N for a new note. Untitled.md appears in the folder with the caret ready. The first line you write becomes its name.

The window title shows the note's name, and the sidebar row of the open note is selected.

See a change

Git, a sync app, a script or a coding agent can change a note while it is open. Dewline marks each change so you can keep it or revert it.

  1. Go back to Welcome.md in the sidebar.
  2. ⌘-click “Show me a change”. A demo writer edits that paragraph, and the new words are marked.
  3. Keep or revert it. The bar under the text offers Keep (⌘↩) and Revert (⌥⌘⌫). ⌘Z undoes either one.

Agents has the rest: every key, the Changes list, and chatting with your own agent.

Open your own folder

  1. Choose File > Open Folder… (⌥⌘O) and pick a folder of Markdown files or an Obsidian vault.
  2. Look at the sidebar. Its top row is the folder's name, with the notes under it. Nothing is imported or copied.
  3. Find a note with ⇧⌘O. Type part of its name and press ↩.

Dewline leaves the vault's .obsidian folder alone. Wikilinks, embeds, tags, callouts and front matter follow Obsidian's syntax.

Quit and come back

Quit with ⌘Q. Every note is already saved. Open Dewline again and your windows, tabs and caret come back where you left them.

To start on the launch window instead, set Settings > General > When Dewline opens.

Switching from Obsidian or Notion

Dewline opens an Obsidian vault as it is, so you can use it instead of Obsidian on your Mac or beside it. From Notion, it suits the notes you want to keep as files.
